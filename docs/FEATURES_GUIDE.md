# FitCoach — User Guide & Feature Reference

FitCoach is your all-in-one fitness companion: it builds your diet plan, coaching schedule,
workouts and body-fat estimates around your goals, gender, height, weight, activity level and
training experience. Every plan automatically recalculates when you update your body
measurements, and the app reminds you about meals, water and workouts throughout the day.

---

## 1. Getting started (log in / sign up)

When you open the app you land on the **Welcome** screen after a quick brand splash.

**Create a new account** — a simple 3-step sign-up:

1. **Step 1 — Account**: Add an optional profile photo (`Take a photo` / `Choose from gallery`,
   then crop to a square), your full name, email and a password (min. 6 characters).
2. **Step 2 — Your body**: gender, age, height (cm), weight (kg), activity level
   (Sedentary / Light / Moderate / Active / Athlete), training experience
   (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced), your preferred workout time, and whether you eat
   vegetarian (and if so, eggs or not).
3. **Step 3 — Measurements** (optional): waist, neck, and hip (females only) in inches.
   These make your body-fat % much more accurate.

Tap **Create account**. You will be asked **once** whether you want **one-tap login** — accept it
and next time you open the app you can get back in with a single tap.

**Log in to an existing account:**

- **Email + password** — enter your details and tap **Log in**.
- **One-tap login** — if enabled, you'll see a big card with your avatar; tap it to verify and
  log in instantly. If your phone has biometrics (fingerprint / Face ID / passcode), you'll be
  asked to confirm with that instead.
- Need a new account? Tap **New client? Create account**.

> Note: For security, the app ends your session if it stays backgrounded for 30 minutes. Your
> one-tap login stays available so getting back in is one tap away.

## 2. Home dashboard

The Home tab is your daily command center:

- **Header** — "Hi {name},", your weight/height/activity summary. Tap it to open your Profile.
- **Daily goal chip** — shows your current phase: **Fat loss phase**, **Lean bulk phase** or
  **Maintenance phase**. Tap it to change your goal:
  - **Cut fat** (20% calorie deficit) / **Build muscle** (lean surplus) / **Maintain**.
  - The app shows a *Recommended* badge based on your body fat.
- **Streak pill** — shows your current workout streak (`🔥 X days streak`). Tap it to see your
  longest streak, total workouts and achievement badges.
- **Daily Target bar** — calories consumed vs. today's calorie target, plus macro pills for
  Protein / Carbs / Fiber. Tap any macro area to open **Today's Progress**.
- **Body & Health Metrics** —
  - **Body Fat** card: your estimated body-fat %, a category badge
    (Essential / Athletic / Fitness / Average / High), and your lean/fat mass + FFMI.
  - **BMI** card: your BMI value, category, and weeks-to-goal.
  - Tapping either opens detailed calculators (see §10 below).
- **Personal Training** card — one-on-one coaching with Yudi: **WhatsApp** and **Call** buttons
  open the trainer directly.

## 3. Diet

The **Diet** tab (`Daily Diet Plan`) shows your personalized daily meal plan:

- **Macro summary** — your daily calorie target and Protein / Carbs / Fiber grams.
- **Scan your food** — an animated banner; tap it to log food by photo, barcode or search
  (see §4).
- **Gym Day / Rest Day toggle** — decide today for each day:
  - **Gym Day** → meals include pre & post-workout snacks.
  - **Rest Day** → workout meals are removed.
  - Switching recalculates the plan and re-schedules your reminders automatically.
- **Water card** — your daily water goal in liters and its calories/macros. Tap it to open
  **Today's Progress** and tick off each 300–500 ml glass.
- **Meals** — each meal card shows an emoji icon, meal name, time, calories, tips and the
  actual food items with grams and macros. Before and after each meal there is a
  **water chip** ("300–500 ml · Drink before/after {meal}") — tap it to check off that glass.

---

## 4. Food scanning & logging (Diet → Scan your food)

The **Scan Food** screen lets you log food in seconds:

1. Pick the **meal** (Breakfast, Morning Snack, Lunch, Pre-workout, Post-workout, Dinner,
   Evening Snack).
2. Use one of:
   - **Camera** — take a photo of your food, then **Analyze Food** (an AI service estimates
     calories & macros).
   - **Gallery** — pick an existing photo of the meal.
   - **Barcode** — enter/scan the item's barcode to look it up.
   - **Search** — type a food name (e.g. "chicken") and pick from matching results with brand
     and kcal shown.
3. Review the result card (serving size, Cal / P / C / F) and tap **Log to {meal}**.
4. You'll see a **"Logged!"** confirmation with progress-to-target bars for calories, protein,
   carbs and fiber, and the meal appears in **Today's Logged Food**.

## 5. Today's Progress

Open it from the Home macro pills or the Diet water card:

- **Food Log** — % of your daily target reached, with bars and remaining calories
  (or "Daily calorie target reached!").
- **Water Intake (300–500 ml)** — tap each slot (`Before/After {meal}`) to mark a glass done.
  A slot is only counted for meals you'll actually have (pre/post-workout meals are excluded).
- **Macro summary** — shows whether today is a **Gym Day** or **Rest Day**, the calorie target,
  and Protein / Carbs / Fiber pills, plus your BMI · BMR · TDEE numbers.

## 6. Workout

The **Workout** tab (`Training Program`) plans your week:

- **Workout split** — pick your weekly training split:
  - **Full Body** (5 days, Beginner default)
  - **Upper / Lower** (4 days, Intermediate)
  - **Two Body Parts** (4-day cycle, Intermediate)
  - **Push / Pull / Legs** (6 days, Advanced)
  - **Single Body Part** (6 days, Advanced bro-split)
  - **Strength Training** (5 days, Powerbuilding/Pro)
  - If you already ticked exercises, the app asks for confirmation before changing the split.
- **Program card** — program name, level and current cycle week.
- **Cardio card** — your weekly cardio sessions/minutes and recommended machine.
- **Warm-up & Cool-down** — quick guidance shown for every session.
- **Weekly schedule** — 7 day cards. Today is highlighted; completed days show a green
  **Done** badge with strikethrough; rest days show "Rest day — recover and come back
  stronger."
  - Expand a day to see each exercise: name, muscle group, rest time, sets × reps, and a
    checkbox.
  - Tap any exercise row to open its **animated demo** (see §11).
  - Tick all exercises in a day to **auto-complete** it — the app marks the day done and
    celebrates with a motivation dialog. This is what grows your **streak** (only completing a
    workout day counts; rest/skip lets a streak fade and it resets if you miss a day).

## 7. Gym Calendar & attendance

The **Calendar** tab (`Gym Calendar`) shows your month at a glance:

- A summary banner tells you whether today is a gym/rest day and whether you attended.
- Days are color-coded:
  - **Green border** — today
  - **Green fill + check** — you attended
  - **Red X** — you skipped
  - **Green dot** — planned gym day
  - **Red dot** — planned rest day
- Tap any day to open the check-in sheet where you can:
  - Plan the day: **Yes, gym day** / **No, rest day**.
  - Record attendance after training: **Attended** / **Skipped**.
- A notification ("Gym today?") reminds you to log your attendance, and tapping that
  notification opens the calendar straight to today.

## 8. Progress tracking

The **Progress** tab lets you record your body over time:

- **Log weight / measurements** — tap the **+** button and enter weight (required) plus any of
  waist, neck, hip, chest, arm and body-fat %.
- **Overall summary** — start weight, current weight, total change (with an arrow) and body-fat %.
  Log at least two entries to unlock this.
- **Weight trend** — a 14-day weight chart (low/high markers).
- **Timeline** — every entry with its measurements; long-press or tap the delete icon to remove
  an entry (confirm first).

---

## 9. Fasting

The **Fasting** tab is a built-in intermittent-fasting timer:

- Pick a protocol: **16:8**, **18:6** or **20:4**.
- Tap **Start {protocol} fast** — a circular ring shows your progress through the fasting
  window (% of window, elapsed/remaining).
- When you reach the target you get a "Fast complete — great discipline!" banner.
- **Stop fasting** ends the window early (with a confirmation) and logs the fast.
- **Past fasts** — your history of completed/ended-early fasts with duration and date.

## 10. Body composition & calculators

From the **Body Fat** or **BMI** cards on Home:

- **Meter** — a color-coded gauge showing your body-fat % (Essential → High), your Body
  Composition split (Lean % / Fat %), Lean Body Mass, Fat Mass and FFMI with a category badge.
- **Calculator** — five ways to measure body fat: **US Navy Tape**, **RFM (Cedars-Sinai)**,
  **3-Site Caliper**, **Smart Scale / DEXA** and **Deurenberg BMI**. Enter the inputs, see a
  live result (body fat %, lean/fat mass, FFMI), and **Save** it to your profile/progress.
- **Simulator** — drag a target body-fat % (5–35%) and see projected fat to lose or lean
  mass to gain, your goal weight, and estimated weeks to reach it.
- **Guide** — a physique reference chart by gender (e.g. male 6–9% "Stage Shredded" up to
  "Above Target"; female equivalents from "Athletic").

## 11. Exercise demos

Open any exercise in the Workout schedule. A bottom sheet shows:

- The exercise name and a color-coded **muscle group** tag.
- An **animated illustration** — start/peak pose images alternating (or motion for
  exercises like Jump Rope, Stationary Bike, Treadmill).
- **Auto-cycling steps** numbered sequentially with the current step highlighted.
- A **pro tip** box.
- Close by tapping anywhere or swiping down.

## 12. Profile & account

The **Profile** tab (cog icon for settings, logout icon on the top-right):

- **Avatar + name + email** — tap the avatar to change your photo (camera or gallery, cropped
  square). Tap your name to edit it. Your user ID is shown as `ID: {userId}`.
- **Info card** — age, height, gender, activity level, experience, diet (Mixed / Vegetarian
  with eggs / Vegetarian egg-free), workout time, and wake-up / gym / sleep times (editable).
- **Measurements** — update weight, waist, neck and hip here. **Your diet and workout plans
  recalculate automatically after you save.**
- **Account options** (from the email row):
  - **Change password** — current + new password.
  - **One-tap login** toggle — on/off for this device.
- **Log out** — ends the session and returns to the Welcome screen.

## 13. Settings

The **Settings** tab (cog on Profile):

- **Appearance** — Follow device / Light / Dark theme.
- **Notifications**:
  - **Enable reminders** master switch.
  - **Meal reminders** — notify X minutes before each meal (default 10, adjustable 5–30).
  - **Workout reminder** — notify X minutes before your workout time (default 20, adjustable
    5–60), only on planned gym days.
  - **Water reminders** — before and after each meal (30 min each side).
  - The app also reminds you to log your gym attendance ("Gym today?" / "Gym tomorrow?").
  - On Android 13+ you'll be asked for the **notification permission**; without it reminders
    can't be shown. Reminders restart automatically after a phone reboot.
- **Account** — change your **profile photo**.
- **Login** — **one-tap login** toggle for this device.
- **Danger Zone** — **Delete Account**: permanently removes your account, stats, streaks, meal
  and workout plans. There is no undo.

---

## 14. Reminders (notifications) at a glance

- **Meal reminders** — "Time for {meal}" before each meal (gym days include pre/post-workout
  meals; rest days use the rest schedule).
- **Water reminders** — "💧 Water reminder — Drink 300–500 ml of water" before & after meals.
- **Workout reminder** — "Workout in {X} min" with anchor times per workout slot
  (morning 6:30 AM, midday 1:00 PM, afternoon 5:00 PM, evening 7:00 PM).
- **Gym check-in reminder** — "Gym today?" / "Gym tomorrow?" to log attendance; tapping it
  opens the calendar.
- Reminders are scheduled for the **next 7 days** and resync whenever the app opens, the
  gym/rest day changes, or you log attendance.

---

## 15. Data & privacy

- All your data (profile, measurements, meal logs, workouts, streaks, fasting history) is stored
  on the FitCoach backend over HTTPS.
- App local storage (session token, one-tap token, theme, notification prefs, water progress)
  lives only on your device.
- You can delete your account at any time from **Settings → Danger Zone**, which removes
  everything.
